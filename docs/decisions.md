| 2026-10-28 | Added (a) distribution checks (`distribution_checks.py`): shape of log size and of model residuals, plus species bootstrap and Spearman checks that do not assume normality; (b) spanning split into ori- vs ter-crossing (`ori_ter.py`): frequency vs chance given size, symmetry, slope vs room alone, joint ori-vs-ter test.
