| 2026-10-21 | Loci whose overlapping calls chain over more than half the chromosome (`wide_locus`) are sized by their largest member call, not the outer span. First model run crashed on loci of size 0 (outer span = whole circle). Empirical S0 on the real species: all 0.51 [0.28, 0.79], local 0.60, spanning 0.75 (events fit, 200 replicates). | The outer span of such a chain is not one inversion's shorter arc. |

Tiers provisional (high = both callers, matching breakpoints; medium = both, overlapping; low = one caller) until the raw-alignment check is added. Family tier = best tier of its loci. | Same tier meaning in every genome; raw-alignment evidence is Phase 6. |
